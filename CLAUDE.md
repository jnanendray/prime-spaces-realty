# Prime Spaces Realty (Demo Site 3)

Fictional real estate agency in Bengaluru. Training project for Bviate Solutions.

## Rules
- Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind v4.
- No secrets and no real phone numbers in the repo. WhatsApp number comes from NEXT_PUBLIC_WHATSAPP_NUMBER.
- All prices and listings are fictional placeholders.
- Branch plus PR for every change. Never push to main after the first commit.
